# QuickBooks: the AI inside it, and the AI that connects to it

Researched 17 October 2026 from vendor documentation. Every line is tagged with its source.

## The short version

- QuickBooks' built-in AI is called Intuit Intelligence. On Australian plans it includes a chat and AI helpers called Accounting AI, GST AI, Customer AI, Finance AI and Project Management AI. [S1] [S4]
- Intuit says its AI features cost nothing extra for now. The chat is in beta and only admin users get the full version. The number of questions each month is capped. [S1] [S18]
- GST AI checks your BAS figures before you lodge and suggests fixes. Intuit says it never changes a transaction or lodges anything itself. [S13] [S14]
- Accounting AI suggests how to code bank transactions, and nothing is posted until you or your adviser approve it. Intuit says you can't turn AI features off one at a time. [S12] [S19]
- Official QuickBooks connections exist for Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, but all three are for US customers only for now. [S36] [S47] [S57]
- The Claude and ChatGPT connections can also create and send invoices. Intuit describes confirmation steps for some actions, such as deleting an invoice or sending a payment reminder. [S36] [S46]
- We found no official QuickBooks connection for Microsoft Copilot. [S54] [S55] [S56]
- Intuit's privacy statement says it may use personal information to train its own AI models. Intuit says its agreements stop OpenAI and Anthropic training their models on customer data. [S29] [S31]
- Intuit may store and process personal information in the United States and other countries outside Australia. [S29] [S32]
- For accountants, QuickBooks Online Accountant retires on 31 December 2026. Its replacement, Intuit Accountant Suite, carries the AI tools and is in beta. [S7]

## AI built in

Intuit's Australian site describes Intuit Intelligence as "the engine that powers AI in your QuickBooks". Through the chat, it says, "you can ask any question and have AI complete tasks for you." [S2]

Intuit's older name, Intuit Assist, still appears on a US Intuit page and in some Australian help article addresses. [S28] [S18]

Intuit says the platform uses "large language models for reasoning, machine learning for prediction, and deterministic code where precision is critical". It also says "Intuit Intelligence can make mistakes." [S26]

Intuit's Australian help says "We don't use GenAI to calculate the numbers in QuickBooks." [S22]

In November 2025 Intuit said OpenAI's models would help power AI agents across its platform. [S64]

### Intuit Intelligence chat (beta)

**What it does**

- Answers plain-English questions from your company's data, such as what your cash flow was last month. [S17] [S18]
- Builds and changes reports, finds transactions, explains why a figure changed, and reads files you upload. [S18]
- Intuit's Australian page says the chat drafts invoices in bulk for your review and sends prioritised payment reminders with your approval. [S3]
- You can describe a repeating task in the chat and give it a schedule. Intuit's examples include a weekly profit and loss digest and invoice reminders. [S3] [S17]
- AI summaries of reports are on Advanced only. [S18]
- Intuit says a QuickBooks product expert or your accountant can join the chat when needed. [S3]

**Where to find it**

- Select the Intuit Intelligence icon, a blue and white keyboard, to open the assistant pane. In full-screen view, past chats are under the Recent tab. [S18]
- The pane opens by itself. To stop that, type a request such as "Disable Intuit Intelligence" or "Stop opening this pane automatically." [S18]

**Which plans have it**

- All four Australian plans list it, marked Beta. [S1]
- Primary and company admin users get the full chat. Other users can only use it for product support. [S3] [S1]
- Intuit's Australian help says each eligible plan includes 25 chat prompts a month. An add-on gives 100 more for $10 a month, and there's no unlimited option. [S18]
- Intuit's accountant page gives different monthly limits for client books: 25 on Simple Start, 50 on Essentials, 100 on Plus and 200 on Advanced. [S7]
- Intuit's update notes say "Each account gets a set amount of capacity each month, and the amount varies by plan." [S17]
- When the prompts run out, the chat stops until your next billing cycle. [S18]
- Intuit says AI features are "currently available at no additional cost". [S1]

**What happens to your data**

- The chat works from your company's live accounting and payments data. [S18]
- Intuit says your thumbs up and thumbs down ratings help the chat learn. [S18]
- Intuit's privacy statement lists "training our artificial intelligence models and other machine learning models" among its uses of personal information. [S29]
- Intuit's responsible AI page says it trains its AI with Intuit's own data, including customer data as described in that privacy statement. [S30]
- The privacy statement says Intuit's service providers include "AI providers, including generative AI providers". [S29]
- The privacy statement describes an opt-out from some automated processing for residents of certain US states. We found no opt-out from AI training. [S29]
- Intuit's help says "Currently, there isn't a way to turn off AI features individually." [S19]

**In Australia**

- Intuit's Australian pricing page and help centre describe it, labelled beta. [S1] [S18]
- Intuit announced the international rollout on 14 October 2026, with a Sydney dateline among others. [S26]
- Intuit says some AI outputs are in English only, and recommends you "review all outputs carefully". [S1]

### Accounting AI (bank feeds and books checks)

**What it does**

- Suggests categories and matches for bank transactions, and learns from how you and your accountant code them. [S5] [S12]
- Groups the transactions it's confident about as "Ready to post", so you can review them and post them together. [S20]
- Sends one link so your accountant, a client or a team member can reply with details and files. It checks the right receipts come back. [S20]
- On Plus and Advanced, suggests the GST code for a transaction. It also reviews your Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss for problems such as duplicates and miscategorised transactions. [S20]
- On Plus and Advanced, it flags anomalies. Intuit says "You stay in control, deciding whether to take action." [S11] [S15]
- Intuit says "All category and matching suggestions must be approved by you or your Advisor before any transaction can be posted." [S12]
- For Plus, Intuit's August 2026 notes describe "Smart Expert Categorisation", where Accounting AI codes transactions for you "with a human reviewing in the background for accuracy". Intuit doesn't say who that person is. [S17]

**Where to find it**

- Ready to post items appear in the Needs attention widget and on the Bank transactions pages. [S20]
- For report insights, go to Reports > Standard reports, open the Balance Sheet or Profit and Loss, select the Insights icon, then Analyse Books with Accounting AI. [S20]
- Work done by Intuit's AI also goes to your business feed for you to review and approve. [S5]

**Which plans have it**

- The bank transaction features are on all four plans. [S20] [S1]
- Collaboration with your accountant (beta) is on Essentials and above. [S12] [S15]
- Anomaly detection, GST code suggestions and report insights are on Plus and Advanced. [S12] [S20]

**What happens to your data**

- Intuit says the AI learns your business as you or your accountant categorise transactions. [S5]
- The training statements under Intuit Intelligence chat apply. We found no separate statement for Accounting AI. [S29] [S30]
- When Intuit launched the AI bank feed, it was opt-in. Intuit said all users would eventually be moved to it automatically. [S12]
- Before turning on Smart Expert Categorisation, Intuit says to check with your accountant or bookkeeper. [S17]

**In Australia**

- Australian pages and help articles describe it. Intuit says it works with Open Banking in Australia. [S12] [S20]
- Intuit's February 2026 notes list improved AI-powered bank feeds and AI extraction from PDF bank statements. [S17]
- Report insights cover "a majority of Balance Sheet and P&L accounts, but not all of them". [S20]

### GST AI (beta)

**What it does**

- Compares the income in your Profit and Loss with the net sales on your BAS for the same period. It lists each transaction that causes a difference and suggests a fix. [S14] [S13]
- Intuit says it's good at catching transactions with no GST rate selected, income posted to an expense account, and income lines carrying a payroll tax code. [S14]
- Intuit says it "highlights and suggests" and "never changes a transaction or lodges anything on its own". [S14]
- You review and approve every correction, then lodge the BAS yourself. [S13]

**Where to find it**

- Go to All apps > GST > Overview. On the To do tab, select the arrow next to Prepare BAS and choose Pre-file check. [S14]
- Pre-file check only appears once you've lodged at least one BAS in QuickBooks. [S14]
- Flagged items also show in the Business Feed. [S13]

**Which plans have it**

- All four plans list it. [S13] [S1] [S6]
- It's labelled beta. Intuit says beta features have limited availability and are subject to change. [S13] [S1]

**What happens to your data**

- It reviews your GST and financial data. The pages we read have no separate data statement for it. [S13]
- Its output was "English only at launch". [S13]

**In Australia**

- It's built for Australian activity statements. Intuit says it helps with a pre-lodgement check for ATO activity statements. [S10] [S14]
- Intuit's page says the agent aims for "alignment with Australian Taxation Office (ATO) standards". It doesn't say the ATO has approved it. [S11]

### Customer AI

**What it does**

- Reads your connected Gmail or Outlook inbox to find possible sales leads, and lists them as Hot or Warm. [S21]
- Suggests next steps, such as a reply or an estimate, and drafts responses. You review a draft and select Send yourself. [S21]
- It keeps watching those conversations and may suggest changes, which you can Confirm or Dismiss. [S21]

**Where to find it**

- The primary admin goes to All apps > Customer Hub > Opportunities, selects the Suggested by AI icon, then Import leads from your inbox. [S21]
- Choose Gmail or Outlook, select Continue twice, then sign in to your email and grant access. [S21]
- To disconnect, go to Integrations > Manage integrations, find the account under My integrations, select the more icon, then Disconnect. [S21]

**Which plans have it**

- Plus and Advanced. [S1] [S15]

**What happens to your data**

- Once connected, it reads 30 days of your email. [S21]
- Intuit's article points you to its privacy settings and makes no other data claim. [S21]

**In Australia**

- Australian pages list it on Plus and Advanced, and Intuit's Australian help describes the setup. [S1] [S21]

## Outside AI that connects

A quick word on the jargon. Intuit's tools reach Claude through MCP (Model Context Protocol), a standard way for an AI assistant to use outside tools. [S33] [S37]

Claude and ChatGPT call these links connectors and plugins. Google's Workspace pages call them integrations. In each case you sign in to QuickBooks and approve access. [S40] [S51] [S58]

### Claude and QuickBooks

**Official?**

- Yes. The "Intuit QuickBooks" connector is made by Intuit QuickBooks and carries Anthropic's verified mark. It was added to Anthropic's directory in March 2026. [S37]
- Anthropic says verified means it tested the connector's tools for quality. It says verification isn't a security audit, and the developer can change the tools after review. [S39]
- Intuit and Anthropic announced their partnership on 24 February 2026. Intuit said its apps were available in Claude on 23 April 2026. [S33] [S35]
- Intuit says the experience works across Cowork, Claude for Enterprise and Claude.ai. [S34]
- Intuit QuickBooks is one of the tools in Anthropic's Claude for Small Business, announced on 13 May 2026. [S41]

**What it can see and do**

- It reads profit and loss, cash flow, the balance sheet, receivables and payables ageing, and sales by customer or product. It can also compare you with similar businesses. [S37] [S36]
- It can make changes. Intuit lists creating, updating, sending, duplicating and deleting invoices and estimates. It can also add customers and products, send payment links and reminders, and import transactions into your general ledger. [S36]
- For payment links and reminders, Anthropic's listing says "always with a clear preview and your confirmation before anything is sent". [S37]
- Intuit says deleting an invoice or estimate needs your confirmation, and a payment reminder goes out "after you confirm". Intuit doesn't say whether its other changes ask first. [S36]
- The listing also offers read-only answers on payroll, plus adding an employee or setting base pay. [S37]
- The connector has 74 tools. [S37]

**What you need**

- You need to be a US customer. Intuit says "The QuickBooks connector for Claude is currently available to US customers only." [S36]
- An active Claude account. Intuit doesn't name a Claude plan. [S36]
- Intuit says you can try it without a QuickBooks subscription by uploading a CSV, PDF or image, or pasting transactions in. [S36] [S37]
- On Claude Team and Enterprise, an Owner adds the connector for the organisation first. [S40]

**Steps to connect** (Intuit's help article, updated 2 October 2026, US customers only)

1. Open Claude.ai and go to Connectors in the left navigation. Anthropic's own help puts this at Customize > Connectors, then the + next to Connectors. [S36] [S40]
2. Find QuickBooks and select Connect. [S36]
3. Sign in to your Intuit account when asked. [S36]
4. Ask Claude a question about your finances. [S36]
5. In a chat, select + (lower left) or type /, hover over Connectors and check QuickBooks is switched on. [S40]

Team and Enterprise Owners first go to Organization settings > Connectors, click Browse connectors, choose the connector and click Add to your team. They can set each tool to Always allow, Needs approval or Blocked. [S40]

For Claude for Small Business, Anthropic says every task is started by you and you "approve before anything sends, posts, or pays". The plugin installs into Claude Cowork. [S41] [S42]

**To disconnect**

- In Claude, go to Customize > Connectors and disconnect QuickBooks. [S40]
- Intuit's article only mentions disconnecting from the Connectors menu as a troubleshooting step. [S36]

**Limits**

- It's for US customers only for now. Intuit says "We're working to bring it to other regions soon." [S36]
- Intuit says "QuickBooks data is not used to train Claude." [S36]
- Intuit says your data "stays within our Intuit systems and is never used to train foundation models". [S45]
- On Claude's Free, Pro and Max plans, Anthropic doesn't train on raw connector content unless it's copied into the chat. Your chats are used for training if you allow it in the Model Improvement setting, or if a chat is flagged for safety review. [S43]
- Intuit tells you to check any transactions Claude creates, and to see a qualified accountant or financial adviser for decisions with financial or legal implications. [S36]
- Anthropic's listing warns: "Only use connectors from developers you trust." [S37]
- Our reading: until Intuit opens the connector outside the US, Claude for Small Business can't use QuickBooks for an Australian business. [S36] [S41]

### ChatGPT and QuickBooks

**Official?**

- Yes. OpenAI lists the "Intuit QuickBooks" plugin, developed by Intuit QuickBooks, in its Finance category. [S48]
- Intuit announced a multi-year partnership with OpenAI on 18 November 2025. [S64]
- Intuit's apps went live in ChatGPT for logged-in Free, Plus and Pro accounts "in the US". [S44]
- Intuit's September 2026 announcement says the plugin brings together "all 50+ QuickBooks tools". [S46]

**What it can see and do**

- Profitability, cash flow, receivables and payables, comparisons with similar businesses, and reports such as profit and loss and balance sheets. [S48]
- It can manage invoices, estimates, payments, customers, products and payroll from the chat. [S48]
- Intuit says it can turn the context in your Gmail conversations into invoices, and you can review and change them "before anything is sent". [S46]
- For payroll, it can onboard new hires and change base pay. Intuit says "You'll review and confirm any change before it's made in QuickBooks Payroll." [S46]
- Intuit's July 2026 update says the sales tools can create, update, send and delete invoices and estimates, set up recurring invoices and send reminders. The payroll tools in that update were for questions only. [S45]

**What you need**

- A QuickBooks Online subscription and a ChatGPT account. Intuit says "This feature is currently available to QuickBooks Online customers in the US." [S47]
- OpenAI says installing or using a plugin depends on your plan, workspace, role and region. [S51]
- Payment features need a QuickBooks Payments account, which Intuit says isn't available outside the US. [S45]

**What we saw from Australia**

- On 17 October 2026, ChatGPT's QuickBooks listing said "We couldn't load this plugin right now" when we opened it from Australia, signed out. [S49]
- Searching ChatGPT's directory for QuickBooks from Australia showed plugins from other developers, but not Intuit's. The same search for Xero showed Xero's own plugin. [S50]
- Our reading: this fits Intuit's US-only statement. We weren't signed in, so we can't say what a signed-in Australian account sees. [S47] [S49] [S50]

**Steps to connect** (Intuit's help article, updated 26 August 2026, US customers only)

1. In ChatGPT, select Plugins. [S47]
2. Search for "Intuit QuickBooks", or go to chatgpt.com/plugins/quickbooks, then select Connect Intuit QuickBooks. [S47]
3. Sign in to your QuickBooks Online account. You're returned to ChatGPT automatically. [S47]
4. From the Intuit QuickBooks plugin, select Start chat. [S47]
5. Ask something simple, such as your open invoices, to check the connection works. [S47]

**To disconnect**

- In ChatGPT, open Settings > Plugins, select the plugin, then select Disconnect. [S52]
- Disconnecting doesn't delete past chats, saved files or memories. [S52]
- Where it's offered, Uninstall plugin removes the plugin. A separately connected server stays connected until you disconnect it. [S53]

**Limits**

- It's for US customers only for now. [S47]
- Intuit says customers' data "stays within the Intuit apps and isn't used to train foundation models". [S44]
- OpenAI says that when ChatGPT sends data through a plugin's server, that service's terms and privacy policy apply. [S53]

### QuickBooks MCP servers and developer tools

**Official?**

- Intuit publishes an open-source MCP server on GitHub, "quickbooks-online-mcp-server", under its intuit organisation. [S60]
- Intuit also runs a hosted MCP server for invited partners, in a pilot. [S61]
- In October 2026 Anthropic's directory added an "Intuit Developer" connector, made by Intuit, for building and testing integrations with Intuit's APIs. [S38]

**What it can see and do**

- The README lists 145 tools across 29 kinds of record and 11 reports. Most kinds of record can be created, read, updated, deleted and searched. Tax codes and tax rates are read-only. [S60]
- The hosted pilot's tools can create, update, send, duplicate, delete and close records such as invoices and estimates. [S61]
- The Intuit Developer connector says it's "not for managing QuickBooks business workflows or production accounting data". [S38]

**What you need**

- An app registered on the Intuit Developer Portal and a one-time sign-in in your browser. The README says Intuit "does not offer per-user API keys". [S60]
- Sandbox testing can run on your own computer. A live company file needs a public https callback address for the first sign-in. [S60]
- The hosted pilot is "By Invitation Only" for partners in Intuit's App Partner Program. [S61]
- The App Partner Program is open to partners based in Australia. Its free Builder tier caps "CorePlus" API calls at 500,000 a month, and the fees shown are US rates. [S62]

**Steps to connect** (README; repository last updated 15 September 2026)

1. Register an app on the Intuit Developer Portal to get a client ID and secret. [S60]
2. Download the server, install it and build it. [S60]
3. Put your credentials in its local settings file. The README keeps that file out of version control. Our advice: never paste these details into a chat or a website. [S60]
4. Run the one-time sign-in against a sandbox company first. [S60]
5. Add the server to your AI app's MCP settings. The README gives an example for Claude Code. [S60]

**To disconnect**

- The README doesn't explain how to remove the connection. [S60]

**Limits**

- The server keeps working without a browser sign-in "until the 100-day refresh window lapses". [S60]
- GitHub shows the licence as Apache 2.0, while the README says MIT. [S60]
- For the hosted pilot, Intuit "reserves the right to change functionality or charge for any MCP server functionality in the future". [S61]
- Anthropic's listing gives the address of Intuit's hosted QuickBooks connector as https://ai-inc.quickbooks.intuit.com/v1/mcp. Intuit says that connector is for US customers only. [S37] [S36]

## Jobs it's good for

## What to watch for

**The outside AI connections are US-only for now**

- The Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini connections for QuickBooks are all described as available to US customers only. [S36] [S47] [S57]
- Intuit says it's working to bring the Claude connector to other regions "soon". It gives no date. [S36]

**Look-alike plugins**

- Searching ChatGPT's directory for QuickBooks from Australia showed plugins from other developers, such as "QBO Connector by Meridian". [S50]
- These aren't Intuit's, and we haven't checked them. Our advice: treat any connection that isn't Intuit's own like any other outside app with access to your books. [S50]

**You can't switch the AI off feature by feature**

- Intuit says "there isn't a way to turn off AI features individually". [S19]
- The chat pane opens automatically. You can ask it to stop opening by typing a request such as "Disable Intuit Intelligence". [S18]

**Caps and costs**

- Chat prompts are capped each month, and Intuit's pages give different caps. An extra 100 prompts cost $10 a month, and no plan is unlimited. [S18] [S7]
- Intuit says AI features cost nothing extra "currently". Its pricing page says pricing can change without notice. [S1]
- Accelerate in Intuit Accountant Suite moves from free to A$113 a month on 20 January 2027. [S7]

**Beta features**

- Intuit Intelligence chat, GST AI, automations and parts of Intuit Accountant Suite are labelled beta. [S1] [S7] [S13]
- Intuit's Australian terms say beta features "may contain errors or inaccuracies that could cause failures, corruption or loss of data", and use them at your sole risk. [S32]

**Training and where your data goes**

- Intuit says it may use personal information to train its own AI models, and that it trains its AI on customer data as described in its privacy statement. [S29] [S30]
- Intuit says its agreements with OpenAI and Anthropic "explicitly prohibit the use of customer data for model training". [S31]
- Intuit's privacy statement says it may store and process your personal information in the United States. Its Australian terms say it may store personal information in countries outside Australia. [S29] [S32]
- Intuit's security page says its equipment is in Intuit-operated and Amazon Web Services data centres, without naming countries. [S8]
- We found no option to keep AI processing in Australia. [S29] [S32]

**Customer AI reads your inbox**

- Customer AI reads 30 days of the Gmail or Outlook account you connect. Connect only the inbox you mean to share. That's our advice, based on Intuit's description. [S21]

**Check the work**

- Intuit recommends you "review all outputs carefully". [S1]
- Intuit says "Intuit Intelligence can make mistakes." [S26]
- Intuit's Australian help says GenAI shouldn't give you legal, tax or investment advice. [S22]
- Intuit's terms say to consult professionals before making important decisions. [S32]

**Figures in the marketing are mostly American**

- Many of the time-saving and accuracy claims on Intuit's Australian pages are footnoted as surveys of US QuickBooks customers. [S1] [S15] [S16]

**Conflicting dates and prices**

- The 70% off offer reads as 6 months in the banner and 3 months in the terms. [S1]
- The Intuit Accountant Suite discount reads as 12 months on one page and 6 months on another. [S7] [S1]
- Check your own subscription settings before relying on either. That's our advice. [S1] [S7]

**For accountants**

- QuickBooks Online Accountant retires on 31 December 2026, and Intuit Intelligence chat isn't available in it. [S7] [S1]
- Questions you ask in a client's books use up that client's prompts. [S7]

**Developer tools can delete records**

- Intuit's open-source MCP server includes delete tools for most kinds of record. [S60]
- Test on a sandbox company first, and read every change before you approve it. That's our advice. [S60]

**Who can switch things on**

- Intuit Intelligence chat: primary and company admin users. [S3] [S18]
- Customer AI: the primary admin connects the inbox. [S21]
- Claude Team and Enterprise: Owners add connectors and set tool permissions. [S40]
- Gemini for work accounts: the Google Workspace admin allows integrations. [S58] [S59]
